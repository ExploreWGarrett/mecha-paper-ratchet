# Mecha Paper Ratchet

This folder is the project home for Mecha Paper Ratchet.

## What this project is
A solo-dev-friendly action roguelite with:
- Ratchet & Clank-style combat energy
- Paper Mario-inspired flat-character readability
- weird comic weapons
- compact, testable prototype scope

## Working priorities
1. Combat feel first
2. Camera readability second
3. Lock-on clarity third
4. Scope control always

## Development stance
- Prefer Godot 4 unless a real blocker appears.
- Keep code under `game/`.
- Keep design notes in the Obsidian markdown structure.
- Favor small playable milestones over broad speculative systems.
- Do not expand into progression, procedural generation, or multiple characters until the combat toybox is fun.

## Current milestone
Build a paper character in a simple 3D arena that can:
- lock onto one enemy
- strafe
- jump
- shoot
- dodge one projectile
- defeat the enemy
