# Side Notes - Callous and Grit

> These are user-supplied side notes. They are intentionally separated from agent research and are not design-approved.

## Callous

**User idea:** an upgrade that lets the player consume less energy.

### Questions to resolve later

- What does **energy** mean in the final game: weapon ammo, ability charge, stamina, heat, or a future resource?
- Does Callous reduce all energy costs or only a named family of actions?
- Is the tradeoff reduced recovery, reduced burst, lower reward value, or no tradeoff?
- Is the name meant to signal emotional hardness, material toughness, or deliberately comic wordplay?
- Should it be a common support upgrade, a character signature, or a mutation ingredient?

### Provisional tags

`energy`, `efficiency`, `support`, `possible-stacking`, `needs-resource-definition`

## Grit

**User idea:** an unvestable upgrade called **Grit**.

The phrase “unvestable” is preserved as written and needs interpretation before implementation. Possible meanings include:

- an upgrade that cannot be removed once taken;
- an upgrade that does not vest immediately and activates after a condition;
- an upgrade whose value is intentionally hard to evaluate;
- a typo or placeholder for another intended term.

### Questions to resolve later

- What does “unvestable” mean here?
- Is Grit a permanent run trait, a delayed payoff, or a risk/reward commitment?
- Does it protect against death, improve recovery, reward low health, or enable stubborn play?
- Should it interact with Callous, energy, damage taken, or repeated failed attempts?

### Provisional tags

`grit`, `commitment`, `delayed-payoff`, `survivability`, `needs-definition`
