## Initial extraction

The source contains several distinct design claims that should not be allowed to blur together:

1. **Fantasy:** being a miner in a collapsing mine.
2. **Core action:** manually excavating downward, beginning with hands and sifting.
3. **Progression:** tools and upgrades unlock new material tiers.
4. **Economy:** recover ore/items, sell them, and buy better capability.
5. **Presentation:** first-person, grounded/realistic, low-to-moderate asset complexity.
6. **Exploration:** collectibles and secret lore are embedded in the descent.
7. **Run structure:** collapse can reset the mine, potentially producing a prestige loop.
8. **Outcomes:** mine-collapse ending and temple-discovery ending.
9. **Specific ideas:** Callous as energy efficiency and Grit as a skill.
10. **Production premise:** existing assets may make the game quick/easy to build.
