# Mecha Paper Ratchet Design Authority Map

This note says which inspirations are allowed to **dominate which subsystem**.

The point is to keep the project from getting pulled equally hard by every cool reference.
A reference pool is useful.
An equal-vote reference pool is chaos.

---

## How to read this note

### Dominant authorities
These are the games that should have the most say when a subsystem is being defined.
If they disagree with weaker references, they usually win.

### Supporting authorities
These can shape details, edge cases, alternate phrasing, or stretch goals.
They help refine the lane, but they should not redefine it.

### Flavor-only contributors
These can contribute:
- naming
- tone
- isolated gimmicks
- visual/combat attitude

They should not override structure, pacing, or anti-exploit priorities.

## 1. Core combat loop authority
### Scope
- lock-on readability
- strafe pressure
- melee/ranged alternation
- readable arena combat
- movement under pressure

### Dominant authorities
- **Risk of Rain 2**
- **Gunfire Reborn**
- **Returnal**

### Why
- **Risk of Rain 2** helps with readable 3D pressure escalation in open combat space
- **Gunfire Reborn** helps with paced ranged pressure and authored action flow around weapon + skill identities
- **Returnal** helps with projectile pressure clarity, tempo, and the idea that movement readability is part of combat design, not separate from it

### Supporting authorities
- **Hades** — for tempo, authored encounter feel, and action readability
- **Roboquest** — for fast gun pressure if the Popper lane grows
- **Above Land: Rhapsody** — for unusual 3D combat language and toy-like confrontation energy

### Flavor-only contributors
- **Noita**
- **Vampire Survivors**

Those are strong elsewhere, but they should not decide the 3D feel baseline.

## Hard weighting rules for now
To keep the project coherent, use these defaults:

1. **If a system question and a tone question conflict, the system authority wins for function, but the tone authority should still shape presentation.**
2. **If a weird weapon idea conflicts with 3D readability, the combat-feel authority wins.**
3. **If a cool synergy conflicts with anti-exploit warnings, do not cut it immediately — first try to gate it. But anti-breakage wins over unchecked escalation.**
4. **If a light-coverage reference conflicts with a strong-coverage reference on structure, the strong-coverage reference wins.**
5. **Above Land: Rhapsody can heavily influence weapon language and combat personality, but not the main upgrade architecture unless stronger source coverage appears later.**

## Bottom line
This project should not sound like every inspiration equally.

Right now the healthiest design authority split is:
- **Risk of Rain 2 / Gunfire Reborn / Hades / Dead Cells** for structure
- **Returnal / Risk of Rain 2 / Gunfire Reborn** for 3D pressure and feel
- **Above Land: Rhapsody / Enter the Gungeon / Isaac** for weapon identity and expressive weirdness
- **exploit-awareness notes / Risk of Rain 2 / Noita / Gunfire Reborn** for anti-breakage discipline

That should keep the game weird, readable, and less directionally muddy.
