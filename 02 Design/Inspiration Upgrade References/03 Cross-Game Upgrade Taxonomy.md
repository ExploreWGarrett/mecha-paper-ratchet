# Cross-Game Upgrade Taxonomy

## Why this file exists
This is the comparison layer: which games lean on raw numerical power, which lean on synergy, and which are most useful for Mecha Paper Ratchet specifically.

## Big current read
The strongest pattern across your inspiration pool is not never use number-go-up upgrades.

It is this:
- pure numbers are fine as fuel
- synergy is what creates memory, build identity, and surprise

## Number-up vs synergy heuristic
### Mostly number-up leaning
These games/systems often use direct stat pushes heavily, even if they still contain synergy:
- Risk of Rain 2 common stat/proc items
- Vampire Survivors passive stat amplifiers
- Binding of Isaac direct tear/damage/range items
- Dead Cells scroll/mutation scaling layers

### Mostly synergy / behavior-rewrite leaning
These games/systems are especially valuable when they make the player ask what new rule did I just create?
- Noita spell + wand assembly
- Hades boon family / duo boon / hammer route shaping
- Enter the Gungeon synergies and gun identity
- Gunfire Reborn hero skill + scroll + weapon trait intersections

### Strongest hybrid references for this project
If the goal is 3D action readability plus upgrade identity, the best blended references in this pool look like:
1. Risk of Rain 2
2. Gunfire Reborn
3. Hades
4. Noita
5. Enter the Gungeon

## Most reusable verbs across the whole pool
Looking across all of these, the most reusable upgrade verbs are:
- mark
- pin / hold / anchor
- recall / recover / reset
- split / ricochet / chain
- cadence / alternation
- route writing / line control
- gather / launch / cash-out

These are the places where generalized upgrades may be more valuable than raw stat boosts.

## Where pure numbers still matter
Pure numbers are still useful when they:
- make a weak but interesting system viable
- create clear common-rarity filler that supports harder-to-find combo pieces
- let the player double down on a favorite route without needing a new mechanic every time
- feed proc ecosystems, cadence thresholds, or mutation breakpoints

## Where pure numbers become weak
They become weak when they:
- never ask the player to change behavior
- do not combine with anything else
- crowd out more authored route-changing ideas
- make weapons feel interchangeable

## Most important conclusion right now
If the current broad upgrade vision still feels off, the likely issue is not too much synergy thinking by itself.
It is more likely one of these:
1. too many planned interactions before the core upgrade verbs are chosen
2. not enough separation between generalized verbs and specialized signatures
3. too much emphasis on capstones before the common/uncommon layer feels grounded

That means the next good planning move is probably:
- identify the generalized verbs you want this game to speak in
- then choose which weapons/characters are allowed to become the weird accents on top
