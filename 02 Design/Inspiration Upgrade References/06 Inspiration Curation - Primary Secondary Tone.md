# Inspiration Curation — Primary, Secondary, Tone-Only

This note is the tighter curation layer for the inspiration pool.

The goal is to stop every reference from exerting equal design pressure.
Some games should strongly shape the project.
Others should only help with:
- naming
- weapon absurdity
- spatial feel
- edge-case thinking

---

## How to read the labels

### Primary
Use these as the main references when making real design calls.
These should have the most weight when deciding:
- upgrade structure
- build logic
- combat pacing
- readable 3D pressure

### Secondary
Useful, recurring support references.
They should influence specific lanes, but not dominate the whole design.

### Tone-only
Useful for flavor, naming, energy, or isolated ideas.
Do not let these override stronger references on system structure unless better source coverage appears later.

## Upgrade structure curation

### Primary

#### Risk of Rain 2
**Why primary:**
- one of the best references for turning simple item growth into emergent build identity
- strong 3D relevance
- high value for scaling, proc logic, and build escalation

#### Gunfire Reborn
**Why primary:**
- one of the cleanest references for layered hero + weapon + support interactions
- very useful for character-route + weapon-route overlap
- strong 3D action relevance

#### Hades
**Why primary:**
- strong authored routing reference
- excellent for curated synergy instead of shapeless item chaos
- good check against over-randomized system sprawl

#### Dead Cells
**Why primary:**
- strong mutation/support bridge reference
- useful middle ground between direct scaling and more authored build identities

### Secondary

#### Noita
**Why secondary instead of primary:**
- extremely valuable for behavior-rewrite thinking
- but more system-wild than the current project probably wants as a day-one structural template
- best used to stretch the imagination, not to define baseline readability rules

#### Vampire Survivors
**Why secondary:**
- excellent for evolution/recipe logic
- less useful as a direct 3D combat pacing model
- strong support reference for explicit upgrade relationships

#### Enter the Gungeon
**Why secondary:**
- useful for weapon identity and synergy callouts
- less central than Risk of Rain 2 / Gunfire / Hades for your broader structure question

#### The Binding of Isaac: Rebirth
**Why secondary:**
- deeply useful for item-identity and mutation thinking
- but risky if treated too directly because its combinatorial chaos can outrun your readability goals fast

### Tone-only

#### Stack Gun Heroes
**Why tone-only for structure:**
- sparse coverage
- good prompt around stacking/mod-shopping/order-sensitive customization
- not strong enough right now to act as a structural authority

#### Mega Bonk
**Why tone-only for structure:**
- useful character/weapon/roster flavor
- not a strong source-backed systems reference from current coverage

#### Above Land: Rhapsody
**Why tone-only for structure:**
- currently more useful for weapon identity and combat language than for documented upgrade architecture

#### Roboquest
**Why tone-only for structure:**
- useful pace reference, but not one of the strongest current authorities for your upgrade logic questions

#### Returnal
**Why tone-only for structure:**
- strong 3D pressure reference, but not the clearest match for the broad item/upgrade architecture problem you are solving first

## Current recommendation for Mecha Paper Ratchet
If you want the project to sound more like itself and less like an average roguelite synthesis, the best weighting right now is:

- let **Risk of Rain 2 / Gunfire Reborn / Hades / Dead Cells** carry more of the upgrade-structure burden
- let **Above Land: Rhapsody / Enter the Gungeon / Isaac** carry more of the weapon-identity burden
- let **Returnal / Risk of Rain 2 / Gunfire Reborn** carry more of the 3D combat-readability burden
- let the **exploit-awareness notes + Risk of Rain 2 + Noita + Gunfire Reborn** carry more of the anti-breakage burden

## Bottom line
The curation pass makes one thing very clear:

**Above Land: Rhapsody belongs in the pool, but not as a top structural systems authority.**

Right now it is strongest as:
- a **primary weapon-identity reference**
- a **secondary combat-feel reference**
- a **tone-only structure reference**
- a **tone-only exploit-risk reference**

That is a healthy place for it.
